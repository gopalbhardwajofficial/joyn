1. SALES & REVENUE VELOCITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  Total Gross Sales (Revenue)
     Description : Total monetary value of all completed sales transactions and 
                   invoices within the selected time window.
     Calculation : Sum of (Invoice Total Amounts).

1.2  Net Realized Sales (Cash Collected)
     Description : Actual liquidity received at counter or bank against sales.
     Calculation : Total Gross Sales - Total Unpaid / Credit Receivables.

1.3  Credit Sales Volume
     Description : Total value of sales conducted on credit / khata terms 
                   awaiting customer settlement.
     Calculation : Sum of (Invoice Total Amount - Amount Received at Billing).

1.4  Total Invoices Count
     Description : Total volume of sales bills / transactions generated.
     Purpose     : Measures billing desk activity and store footfall throughput.

1.5  Average Order Value (AOV)
     Description : The average revenue generated per customer transaction.
     Calculation : Total Gross Sales / Total Invoices Count.

1.6  Sales Growth Rate (Period-over-Period)
     Description : Percentage change in total revenue compared to the equivalent 
                   prior period (e.g., this month vs last month, this year vs last year).
     Calculation : ((Current Period Sales - Prior Period Sales) / Prior Period Sales) * 100.

1.7  Payment Method Distribution
     Description : Percentage and monetary breakdown of payment tenders:
                   - Cash
                   - UPI / QR Payments
                   - Bank Transfer (NEFT / IMPS / RTGS)
                   - Cheque


2. WORKING CAPITAL & LIQUIDITY MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Total Accounts Receivable (You'll Get / Debtors)
     Description : Total outstanding balance owed by all customers / credit parties.
     Purpose     : Critical for working capital management and credit risk monitoring.

2.2  Total Accounts Payable (You'll Give / Creditors)
     Description : Total outstanding liabilities owed to vendors and distributors.
     Purpose     : Ensures timely vendor settlements and cash flow forecasting.

2.3  Net Working Capital Gap
     Description : Difference between receivables and payables.
     Calculation : Total Receivables - Total Payables.

2.4  Cash in Hand
     Description : Real-time balance of physical currency available in the cash drawer.
     Calculation : Opening Cash + Cash Inflows (Sales / Payments In) - Cash Outflows (Purchases / Expenses).

2.5  Reconciled Bank Balance
     Description : Aggregated real-time balance across integrated business bank accounts.

2.6  Accounts Receivable Aging Analysis
     Description : Categorization of pending customer dues by overdue duration:
                   - 0 to 30 Days (Current / Healthy)
                   - 31 to 60 Days (Attention Needed)
                   - 61 to 90 Days (Overdue)
                   - 90+ Days (High Risk / Bad Debt Risk)


3. PROFITABILITY & OPERATIONAL MARGINS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Gross Profit
     Description : Direct profit generated after deducting Cost of Goods Sold.
     Calculation : Total Sales Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold (Purchase Rate * Qty).

3.2  Gross Margin Percentage
     Description : Percentage of sales revenue retained as gross earnings.
     Calculation : (Gross Profit / Total Sales Revenue) * 100.

3.3  Total Operating Expenses (OpEx)
     Description : Aggregate of all direct and indirect business operational overheads 
                   (Rent, Salaries, Utilities, Logistics, Maintenance, Marketing).

3.4  Net Operating Profit
     Description : Final bottom-line business profit after deducting all expenses.
     Calculation : Gross Profit - Total Operating Expenses.

3.5  Net Profit Margin Percentage
     Description : Percentage of top-line revenue converted into bottom-line profit.
     Calculation : (Net Operating Profit / Total Sales Revenue) * 100.


4. INVENTORY VALUATION & STOCK INTELLIGENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Total Stock Valuation at Purchase Cost
     Description : Total monetary value of current warehouse/store inventory at acquisition cost.
     Calculation : Sum of (Item Current Quantity * Unit Purchase Price).

4.2  Potential Stock Valuation at Retail Selling Price
     Description : Projected revenue realizable upon selling 100% of current inventory.
     Calculation : Sum of (Item Current Quantity * Unit Selling Price).

4.3  Unrealized Inventory Gross Margin
     Description : Potential gross margin locked in current inventory.
     Calculation : Potential Stock Valuation (Selling) - Total Stock Valuation (Purchase).

4.4  Total Active Catalog SKUs
     Description : Total number of distinct active product items maintained in inventory.

4.5  Out-of-Stock (Stockout) Count & Alerts
     Description : Number of items with zero available inventory.
     Impact      : Highlights missed revenue opportunities and prevents stockouts.

4.6  Low Stock Warning Count
     Description : Number of items whose available quantity has fallen below the 
                   configured minimum threshold level.
     Purpose     : Generates automated purchase reorder triggers.

4.7  Top 10 Fast-Moving / Most Selling Items
     Description : Ranked list of items generating highest unit volumes and revenue.
     Metrics     : Quantity Sold, Total Revenue Generated, Profit Margin Contribution.

4.8  Dead Stock / Slow-Moving Items (Non-Moving Inventory)
     Description : Products with zero sales velocity over 30, 60, or 90 days.
     Key Metric  : Locked Capital Value (Total monetary value tied up in unsellable stock).


5. CUSTOMER & VENDOR INTELLIGENCE

5.1  Top VIP Customers

5.2  Top Vendor / Supplier Partners
     Description : Ranked list of procurement partners by purchase volume and order frequency.

5.3  Customer Repeat Purchase Rate
     Description : Percentage of customers who return for multiple purchases within a period.

5.4  Customer Payment Discipline Index
     Description : Tracking of average days taken by parties to settle credit invoices.
